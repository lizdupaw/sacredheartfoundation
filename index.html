About the Foundation

Rooted in Compassion. Driven by Community.

Since 1984, Sacred Heart Foundation has connected the generosity of our community with the needs of the patients and families we serve. Through philanthropy, we help advance patient care, fund innovative technology and lifesaving equipment, support education and programs, and strengthen the future of healthcare across our region.

Together, we’re carrying forward a legacy of compassionate giving and helping make exceptional care possible right here at home.
